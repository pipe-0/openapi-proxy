This policy covers the hosted service at openapi-proxy.app. If you run openapi-proxy on your own Cloudflare account, nothing reaches us, and whoever runs that instance is responsible for it.

## Who is responsible

Pipe0 GmbH (Schweiz), Quellenstrasse 34, 8005 Zürich, Switzerland. Email: [support@openapi-proxy.app](mailto:support@openapi-proxy.app).

## What we store

- **Your account.** When you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address, profile picture and Google account id.
- **Sessions.** While you are signed in we store a session, with the IP address and browser it was started from. Sessions end after 30 days at the latest.
- **Your workspace.** The people in it, the email addresses of people you invite, your projects (the URL of each OpenAPI document and a copy of the document), your variants, and your settings, including which change notices you want.
- **Source credentials.** Request headers you give us for fetching a document that is not public. They are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before they are stored, are never shown again, and are only sent to the origin of that document.
- **Emails we send you.** Invitations and notices about changes in your documents, sent through Cloudflare.
- **Request logs.** Cloudflare records requests to the service, including IP addresses and URLs, for operation and security.
- **Messages you send us**, for example to ask for a Pro plan, and the billing records of a Pro plan.

Variants are public by design: anyone with the URL of a variant can read it, and requests for it are logged like any other.

We do not use analytics or advertising. The only cookies are the ones that keep you signed in. Your browser also remembers your color scheme and the project you last opened, locally.

## Why

- To provide the service you signed up for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
- To keep it secure and to find and fix problems, which is our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
- To keep billing records as tax law requires (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).

## Who processes it for us

| Processor | What for |
| --- | --- |
| Cloudflare, Inc. | Hosting, database, request logs, sending emails |
| Google LLC | Signing in |
| Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd. | Payments for the Pro plan |

We do not sell data, and we share it with no one else unless the law requires it.

Cloudflare and Google are based in the United States, and data may be processed there. Such transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the recipient is certified under it, and otherwise on the Standard Contractual Clauses of the European Commission.

## How long

- Account and workspace data: until you ask us to delete your account, then within 30 days.
- A project's stored document and credentials: until you delete the project.
- Sessions: until you sign out, and 30 days at the latest.
- Request logs: as long as Cloudflare keeps them, a few days.
- Billing records: as long as tax law requires, usually ten years.

## Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict its processing, to object to it, and to receive it in a portable format. Write to [support@openapi-proxy.app](mailto:support@openapi-proxy.app). We answer within one month.

You can also complain to a data protection authority: the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC), or the authority of the EU country where you live.

## Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we update the date above and tell signed-in users by email.
