Your backend generates one OpenAPI document for everything it does. openapi-proxy fetches that document, keeps the parts each audience should see, and serves each selection at a URL of its own. When your API changes, the served documents change with it.

## Three things

- A **workspace** is the people who share the projects. Signing in gives you one, and you invite colleagues into it.
- A **project** is one API as its readers know it: one OpenAPI document fetched from a URL, or several merged into one. A document can be public, or behind credentials that the proxy sends for you.
- A **variant** is a selection of that document's operations, served at `/v/<project>/<variant>`. Your public docs read one, your SDK generator another.

A variant keeps the operations you chose and exactly what they reference: schemas, parameters, responses, security schemes. Nothing else from the source ends up in it. It can be served as OpenAPI 3.0 or 3.1, whichever its reader needs.
